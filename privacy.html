Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version

Glowreel is a screen recording app for the Mac. It does not collect any personal data. Your recordings stay on your Mac, and nothing is uploaded, shared or tracked.

What Glowreel stores on your Mac

  • Recordings: the screen or window you choose in Apple's screen picker, saved inside Glowreel's own folder on your Mac.
  • Mouse positions and clicks: recorded only while you are recording, so Glowreel can zoom in on clicks and draw a smooth cursor. Glowreel never records your keyboard.
  • Settings: your chosen look (background, zoom, cursor) and export settings.
  • Exports: saved only where you choose in the Save window.

What Glowreel does not do

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • No uploading of recordings, mouse data or settings. Glowreel has no servers.

Purchases

Glowreel Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment under Apple's privacy policy. Glowreel only learns whether Pro is unlocked; it never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Internet use

The only network use is Apple's App Store, to show the Pro price and check purchases.

Deleting your data

Delete recordings in Finder, or remove everything by deleting Glowreel and the folder ~/Library/Containers/app.glowreel.Glowreel.

Children

Glowreel collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
