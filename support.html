Recording

  • Start: click the big red button and pick a screen or window in Apple's picker. Shortcut: ⇧⌘R.
  • Stop: click the red stop button with the timer in the menu bar.
  • No permission needed: Glowreel uses Apple's own screen picker, so you choose exactly what is recorded.

Auto zoom and cursor

  • No zoom: auto zoom follows your clicks. Click while recording, and check that Zoom in on clicks is on in the editor.
  • Cursor in the wrong place: if you recorded a single window and moved it while recording, the cursor can drift. Record the whole screen instead.

Exporting

  • Click Export, pick a size and format, then choose where to save.
  • H.264 plays everywhere. HEVC files are about half the size.
  • Free exports go up to 1080p and include a small "Made with Glowreel" badge.

Glowreel Pro

  • A one-time purchase through the App Store. It removes the badge and unlocks 4K and original-size export.
  • New Mac: open Glowreel, then the app menu, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
